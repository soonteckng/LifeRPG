# Custom duration, next step and daily achievements

This follow-up uses the existing installed Android version 7 through the preview EAS Update channel. It changes JavaScript only. No new native module, dependency, permission or live database change is needed.

Use an existing account. Do not create accounts with invented addresses or repeatedly request email resets: the Supabase project has received a bounce warning. Nothing in this retest needs a new signup or email.

## Changed behavior

- Home's duration no longer comes from old completed-session history. A cold app launch selects the last explicitly chosen 30/60-minute preset, initially 30 minutes. Before Custom has been saved, its editor opens with the current preset. After saving Custom, the editor remembers that exact duration for this account, including after a restart. Editing then dismissing without “Use this duration” does not change it.
- Find your next step has six direction cards: Learning, Work & projects, Creativity, Everyday life, Wellbeing and Free focus. The former Suggestions/Free focus tabs are removed. The starting-point choices follow the selected direction. Free focus has its own open-ended starter, starts with Everyday focus and leaves area editing available on Home. Selection haptics follow the existing Settings toggle.
- The timer still derives elapsed time from its saved deadline. Display refreshes now align with whole-second boundaries instead of being limited to fixed 200 ms samples. A busy phone can still delay a rendered frame; delayed frames catch up without moving the real deadline or granting extra time.
- After Done closes a confirmed result and its timer page, the first session of the current local day has a separate animated achievement moment. Reaching the daily goal has its own moment. If one session proves both, one moment shows both. Continue fades the moment out over 200 ms before returning to the app; with Reduce Motion it dismisses immediately. No extra XP, currency or reward is added. These local acknowledgements do not repeat when the result is reopened. Pending saves and old recovered days cannot claim today's achievement.
- After the app process is cleared, old notification actions continue to open the restored timer's controls. Direct notification actions are supported while the process remains alive. This update does not weaken the cold-recovery session checks.
