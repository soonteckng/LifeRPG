# Calm focus experience

LifeRPG supports students and anyone making time for learning, work, creative practice, everyday tasks or a quiet screen-free activity. Five directions expose ten curated starting points without replacing free focus or personal quests. Existing study choices and saved suggestion markers remain readable.

Focus areas are simple effort categories. General displays as Everyday focus, Knowledge as Learning, Fitness & Health as Wellbeing, and Grooming & Vitality as Personal care. Life Admin remains Life admin. These labels do not rewrite saved subjects, IDs or XP. Custom area names remain unchanged. A work or creative suggestion uses a real matching saved area when available; otherwise it honestly uses Everyday focus. Users can adjust the area before starting.

Profile holds one milestone collection entry. The collection leads with one milestone within reach, an earned collection and one next step in each of five tracks. Show all reveals the full eighteen. Six existing milestone IDs remain; completed seconds and distinct historical focus days determine progress. No new XP, Gold or claims are introduced. Quiet days cannot remove already recorded growth. Progress keeps analytics and session history.

A near-black base and raised charcoal panels replace blue surfaces. A warm stone accent marks choices and actions; muted category colors remain distinct. Body, quiet labels and actions meet checked contrast thresholds on the actual surface tokens. Native glass/reduced transparency and reduced motion behavior remain supported. Splash/native backgrounds follow the palette on the next installed rebuild.

The seven-page setup and six-message tour keep their existing timing, Back/Next, dynamic full-card measurement and covered return to Home. Wording describes Focus areas and the single Profile collection. Home reserves the measured dock only; no viewport fillers or added fake tour spacing are used. The existing Session scroll-wheel timer is preserved; Home Custom still accepts exact minutes and seconds.

Design references: [Apple Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode), [Apple Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding). Browser previews of the actual Home/Profile/collection components verify static layout, not native phone frame pacing or background alerts. Account/session behavior and native animation tests remain part of acceptance.
